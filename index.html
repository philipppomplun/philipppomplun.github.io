How I work

From messy problem to measured result

The process behind every case study above — scaled up or down depending on the project.

01

Understand

Analytics, heatmaps, session recordings and stakeholder talks to find where users actually struggle.

→ Problem statement
02

Frame

Turn insights into a clear hypothesis and success metrics, aligned with PM and dev before designing.

→ Hypothesis + KPIs
03

Design

Flows, wireframes and high-fidelity UI — explored in multiple directions, accessible and responsive.

→ Prototype
04

Validate & ship

A/B test or usability test, then hand-off and support through implementation.

→ Shipped + measured
